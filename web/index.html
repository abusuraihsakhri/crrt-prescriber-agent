Enter measurements and select Calculate.

KDIGO 2012 describes a delivered effluent interval of 20–25 mL/kg/h in adults with AKI. Effluent dose is a proxy, not a direct measurement of solute clearance.

Effluent dose = volume / (weight × duration)

Net balance = intake − other output − net CRRT patient removal
